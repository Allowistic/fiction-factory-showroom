# Cantina pieces: credits
- cantina_bar, cantina_back_bar, cantina_stools, cantina_booth and all 10 cantina_prop_* models: original procedural geometry by Clay (Blender 4.3; build2.py / props2.py). No Star Wars IP.
- Surface textures (baked into each *_atlas.jpg), all from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com), CC0 1.0:
  - Wood066 (walnut, tinted darker)
  - Metal032 (tinted to aged brass)
  - Leather037 (tinted to deep oxblood)
  - Metal046B (dark steel)
  Dark/amber glass is a procedural shader.
- ent_center_bar: Vincent's Marcos Bini design (Mozaik/SketchUp export), baked in Blender. The bake HDRI "Lebombo" is from Poly Haven (CC0) and is not embedded.
- Dropped from the previous pass: KayKit Space Base Bits / Restaurant Bits (CC0). Their file names are kept, but the contents are replaced with realistic models.
- Not used: the CC-BY "Beer Tap", ShareTextures, and any CC-BY Sketchfab model.
- cantina_robot_bartender ("Tapper"), cantina_robot_dex (Dex, the DJ) and cantina_dj_booth (2026-10-17): original procedural geometry by Clay (Blender 4.3; cantina_src/robots.py, build_robots.py, build_booth.py). Generic sci-fi, no Star Wars or other IP. The only outside assets are the same ambientCG CC0 textures listed above (Metal032, Metal046B, Leather037, Wood066). cantina_dj_screen_placeholder.jpg is generated (PIL) and meant to be replaced at runtime.
