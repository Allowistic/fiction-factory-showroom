# Retro Sci-Fi Cantina — Free 3D Resources (CC0 / CC-BY only)
Scouted 2026-10-16 by Radar for Clay (3D). Public repo, so only CC0 or CC-BY. No Star Wars IP.
Excluded because already known: Poly Haven, ambientCG, Quaternius, Kenney, Poly Pizza.

**How I checked:** Sketchfab licenses and face counts come from each model's own record in the Sketchfab API (`license.label`, `faceCount`), and every one was downloadable. Sketchfab offers glTF/GLB (auto-converted) plus the uploader's original format on every downloadable model. "Faces" is Sketchfab's triangle count. Site-wide licenses (KayKit, textures, HDRIs) were read on the source page.
**CC-BY credit line format:** `"<Title>" (<URL>) by <author> on Sketchfab, licensed under CC BY 4.0.` Put it in the repo's CREDITS.md.

## Top picks
1. **KayKit Space Base Bits** (Kay Lousberg). https://kaylousberg.itch.io/space-base-bits . CC0, OBJ/FBX/GLTF, low-poly with a shared atlas. Gives you modular sci-fi walls, crates and props in one style.
2. **KayKit Restaurant Bits**. https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-restaurant-bits . CC0, OBJ/FBX/GLTF. Bar food, drinks, tables, stools, kitchen props. Same art style as #1.
3. **dradnon Modular Hallway set** (Straight Type 01). https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/671ccfbf33ad422f85ba382af3238c47 . CC-BY, 720 tris. Ships with siblings (see §3).
4. **Low Poly Sci Fi Vents With Pipes** (vivianindatub). https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/3e0d8b8f04bf47caa629bca0e4e949bb . CC-BY, 1,523 tris.
5. **Bar Stool** (commonspence). https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/518fe01989904bf48a4a04980bc578af . CC-BY, 396 tris.
6. **Round metal table** (AnKhe777). https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/1afe6a3e6faa45eb9e65aad7f3bba22c . CC-BY, 192 tris.
7. **Open HDRI, Indoor category**. https://openhdri.org/c/indoor . CC0 (see https://openhdri.org/license). Includes Fluorescent Lit Machinery Room and Historic Flour Mill.
8. **3DTextures.me / TextureCan / ShareTextures**. CC0 PBR texture libraries (§4).

## 4. CC0 PBR textures (beyond ambientCG)
- **3DTextures.me**. https://3dtextures.me/about/ . The site says "All textures on this site are licensed as CC0." Good for sci-fi panels and metals.
- **TextureCan**. https://www.texturecan.com/terms/ . "under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0" license. Has Metal, Fabrics, Plastic and other categories.
- **ShareTextures**. https://www.sharetextures.com/about . Assets are "licensed as CC0 when you download them on sharetextures.com". Note the site has a "Limited CC0 Application" terms page, so read it before you rehost.
Look in these for worn metal, painted panels, rubber floor, leather and glass. I checked the site-wide licenses, not individual textures.

## 5. HDRIs (beyond Poly Haven)
- **Open HDRI**. https://openhdri.org/c/indoor . CC0, confirmed at https://openhdri.org/license . Indoor picks: Fluorescent Lit Machinery Room, Historic Flour Mill, Ludwikowice Underpass, Hilbert's Mill Conference Room. Up to 29K EXR, so downscale to 1–2K for WebXR.
- Note: MorfVision (morfvision.store) mostly mirrors Poly Haven HDRIs and mixes CC0 with CC-BY, so it adds little.

## 6. Threads and tips (WebXR optimization)
- Justin Kamen on X (2026-04-13): "npx gltf-transform optimize input.glb output.glb --compress meshopt". https://x.com/justindkamen/status/2043721673308848386
- Luke Maj on X (2026-05-06): a game-jam pipeline using Draco plus KTX2 (toktx via KHR_texture_basisu). https://x.com/lukemaj_/status/2051954622261022929
- Arseny Kapoulkine (meshoptimizer author) on X (2026-06-08): KHR_meshopt_compression v1 gives extra gains, but glTF-Transform doesn't support it yet. https://x.com/zeuxcg/status/2063836641224245248
- Vectreal on X (2026-04-20): mesh simplification with glTF-Transform plus KTX2/Basis in a web worker. https://x.com/vectreal/status/2046290295403458897
- three.js forum, "Incredible Compression on a GLB": https://discourse.threejs.org/t/incredible-compression-on-a-glb/56996
- Suggested pipeline: atlas textures (the KayKit packs already share one), bake lighting in Blender to a lightmap, then `gltf-transform optimize --compress meshopt --texture-compress ktx2`.
